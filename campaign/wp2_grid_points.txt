# The reduced model depends on exactly two parameters -- Ja is a pure energy
# scale -- so this two-dimensional grid IS the full (Ja, Jb, Jc) scan, with the
# scale restored only when converting to Kelvin.  The inverse map is
#   Jb/Ja = -2 Jpm + 2 Jpmpm,   Jc/Ja = -2 Jpm - 2 Jpmpm,
# and every point below sits inside the fit domain of material_fit_config.json
# (|Jb|,|Jc| <= Ja and Jb >= Jc, the latter automatic for Jpmpm >= 0).
#
# Coverage spans BOTH signs of Jpm.  Two of the three published seeds are at
# positive Jpm, and all 114 pre-existing plane points are at negative Jpm, so
# the positive half has never been scanned.  It is also where the best
# certified two-peak ratio so far lives: QMC_ordered at (+0.071, 0.038) gives
# 0.111, against the negative side's certified ceiling of ~0.098.
#
# (0.000, 0.000) is omitted: with no transverse coupling at all there is no
# band and no dynamics to measure.
