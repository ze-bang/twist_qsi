# Active manuscript figures

- `fig_fcc32_thermodynamics.pdf` — paired periodic/masked FCC-32 ice-band heat
  capacity at the sign-free benchmark and over the negative-coupling scan.
- `fig_fcc32_dssf.pdf` — periodic and transport-masked
  `Szz(q,omega)` at all eight FCC-32 momenta.
- `fig_loop_ratios.pdf` — measured `K8/K6`, `K10/K6`, and `K12/K6` against
  coupling, with analytic leading-order ratios and deeper-virtual-space checks.

The first two are produced by `campaign/make_fcc32_paper_figures.py`; the last
is produced by `campaign/make_clean_paper_figures.py`. Their exact inputs are
listed in `../DATA_PROVENANCE.md`. Other PDFs here are archived campaign assets
and are not referenced by `../main.tex`.
